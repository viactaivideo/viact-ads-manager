# viAct approved ad claims

Canonical wording set by the account owner. Ad copy must use these exact
strings — a variant fragments the message across ads a single searcher may see
more than once.

| Claim | Exact wording | Length |
|---|---|---|
| AI modules | `200+ Safety Modules` | 19 |
| Projects | `500+ Projects Worldwide` | 23 |

Both fit Google's 30-character headline limit. In a description, embed the
string verbatim: *"Trusted Across 500+ Projects Worldwide With Enterprise-Wide
Safety Management."*

## Both claims are mandatory in every ad

Every responsive search ad and every Performance Max asset group in an active
Search or PMax campaign must carry both strings, and no headline may be
duplicated within the same ad. Video and remarketing campaigns are out of
scope.

Both were at Google's headline cap, so each addition displaced an existing
headline. No asset in the account carries a performance label — all 640 read
NOT_APPLICABLE — so "low performing" cannot be read from data. Headlines were
displaced on the two grounds that are evidenced: an exact or near duplicate of
another headline in the same ad, or a claim that cannot be substantiated.

## Claims that must not be used

Removed from every active Search and PMax campaign on 18 Aug 2026. Four
categories, applied uniformly to headlines, long headlines, descriptions,
sitelinks and callouts:

**Superiority** — a comparative rank with nothing establishing it.

    No.1 Construction Surveillance    Singapore's Leading VSS
    No.1 Safety VSS                   No. 1 Construction VSS
    No.1 AI-enabled Construction VSS  Singapore's Leading AI VSS
    No.1 Singapore VSS                No.1 VSS Singapore
    No. 1 HK 4S System                Best Construction Safety App
    Leading EHS Platform KSA          Dubai's Top Safety Platform
    Trusted by Top SG Fleets
    Most Advanced VSS, Adopted by Singapore's Leading Real Estate Developers
    Most Awarded AI Solution Built For Singapore Construction...
    Best Construction Safety App in Singapore: 90% Less Accidents...

**Absolute** — a guarantee of an outcome that cannot be guaranteed.

    100% Safety Compliance            Ensure 100% PPE Compliance
    Lowest Price Guarantee            Zero Blind Spot

**Unsubstantiated statistic** — a percentage or multiple with no source.

    90% / 85% Fewer Accidents         95% Better Workflow View
    95% Workflow Clarity              95% Workflow Transparency
    90% Better Monitoring             90% Better Surveillance
    90% Accident Reduction            90% Less Manual Check(s)
    90%+ Accuracy Onsite              90% Fewer Breaches
    90% Fewer Safety Breaches         90% Fewer Vehicle Incidents
    92% Fewer PPE Violations          80% Fewer Repeat Breaches
    80% Less PPE Violations           70% Cheaper Monitoring
    60% Faster Reporting              60% Less Reporting Time
    Cut Incidents by 90%              10x Audit Readiness
    10x Lower Insurance Cost
    Reduce Manual Inspections By Up To 90%...
    Proven 90% Reduction in Safety Breaches
    Cut vehicle incidents 90%...

**Unsubstantiated endorsement** — implies a body approved the product.

    MOM-Approved Site VSS

`MOM-Compliant Site Safety` and `Singapore MOM Compliant` were kept: stating
compliance with a regulation is a different claim from being approved by the
regulator, and both remain live.

Any of these may be reinstated once the underlying evidence is confirmed.
